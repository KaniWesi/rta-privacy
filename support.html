Before you write

It helps to know which phone you are on, which version of Ṛta, and what you were doing when it went wrong. Ṛta keeps no logs and sends nothing anywhere, so your description is genuinely all there is to go on.

Your birth details

They live only on your phone. You can change them any time from the moon button in the top right, and deleting the app removes them for good. Nobody, including us, can look them up or restore them.

If a reading looks wrong

The Pañcāṅga is calculated on your device from the birth date, time and place you entered, so the usual cause is a detail entered differently than intended. Birth place is used only to work out what your birth time means in universal terms, so a neighbouring town on the same clock gives an identical result. If you have checked all that and it still disagrees with an almanac you trust, write in and say which almanac.
