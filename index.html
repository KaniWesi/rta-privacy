Privacy Policy

Ṛta · last updated 10 September 2026

Ṛta collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Ṛta (“the app”) is published by Axl Maas. This policy describes what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does very little with it.

What the app stores, and where

The app asks for your birth date, birth time and birth place. These are used to calculate your birth Nakṣatra, which personalises the reading you see.

All of it is saved only in the app's own local storage on your device. There is no account, no login, no sign-in of any kind, and no server belonging to us that your details could be sent to. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Alongside your birth details, the app stores your reminder preference and two small display settings. All of these are local in the same way.

What the app sends over the network

Nothing.

The calculation of the Pañcāṅga runs entirely on your device, using an astronomy library bundled inside the app. Birth-place search reads a city list that is also bundled inside the app, so typing a place name does not query any online service. The readings, glossary and practice descriptions are all included in the download.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking software of any kind. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it holds no identifier that could be used to do so.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app asks your phone for permission to post notifications and schedules them on the device itself. There is no push service involved and nothing about your reminders leaves the phone.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — only if you turn reminders on.
  • Running scheduled reminders — so a reminder you have set survives a restart and arrives on time. This comes with the notification component.
  • Internet access — declared by the app framework. The app makes no network requests on its normal path.

Children

Ṛta is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Data you can request or delete

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to send you or delete on your behalf. To remove everything the app has stored, clear the app's data in your phone's settings or delete the app.

Not medical advice

Ṛta is for personal interest and wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat or predict any medical condition, and nothing in it is a substitute for advice from a qualified professional. The suggested āsana are general practice suggestions. Practise within your own limits and consult a doctor before starting new physical activity if you have any reason for concern.
